Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

This policy explains what Qintara Corp ("Qintara", "we", "us") collects when you use qintaracorp.com or book a call with us, what we do with it, and how you can get it removed.

What we collect

  • What you give us. Your name, email address, mobile number, company, and anything you type into a booking form or tell our phone receptionist.
  • How you found us. Standard web log data such as pages viewed, referring site, browser type, and approximate location from your IP address.

We do not collect payment card details on this site, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Why we use it

  • To schedule, confirm, remind you about, and follow up on a call you booked.
  • To answer a question you asked us.
  • To keep the site working and to understand which pages are useful.
  • To meet a legal or regulatory obligation.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number when booking, we use it to send appointment confirmations and reminders for that booking. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not sell or share mobile numbers. No mobile information will be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party for any purpose.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that make our business run, and only so they can perform that service for us. Today that includes our scheduling and CRM provider, our messaging and telephony provider, our email provider, and our hosting and analytics providers. They are bound to use it only on our instructions.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights, safety, or property. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and contact records for as long as we have an active relationship with you, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes. Ask us to delete your record and we will, unless we are required to keep it.

How we protect it

Access is limited to people who need it. Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take it seriously and we will tell you promptly if something goes wrong that affects you.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop text messages.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Email us to see, correct, or delete what we hold about you.

If you live somewhere with additional privacy rights, such as California or the EU, those rights apply and we will honour them. Contact us using the details below.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep the site working and to measure traffic. Your browser can block them; the site will still function.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
