How consent is collected

There is exactly one way a mobile number enters our messaging program: the person types it into our booking form themselves and ticks a consent checkbox. We do not buy, rent, append, or otherwise acquire phone numbers, we do not collect mobile numbers over the phone or on paper, and we do not text a number obtained any other way.

The booking form at qintaracorp.com/book/

Anyone can reach the consent language in a few seconds, with no account and no login:

  1. Open https://qintaracorp.com/book/. A disclosure headed “About text messages” is printed directly beneath the calendar and is visible on that first screen.
  2. In the calendar, click any highlighted date, then click any available time slot.
  3. Click the Select button that appears next to the chosen time. The details form opens.
  4. The form asks for first name, last name, phone, and email. Directly above the Schedule meeting button is the consent checkbox shown verbatim below.

The exact text of that checkbox, as it appears on the form:

I agree to receive appointment confirmations and reminders by text from Qintara Corp at the number I provide. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy at qintaracorp.com/privacy and Terms at qintaracorp.com/terms.

The checkbox is unchecked by default and must be ticked deliberately by the person booking. It is not pre-selected, and consent to receive texts is not a condition of booking the call or of buying anything from us.

The disclosure printed beneath the calendar on the same page reads:

By giving us your mobile number when you book, you agree to receive appointment confirmations and reminders by text from Qintara Corp at that number. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We do not sell or share your mobile number or your consent to text messaging with anyone.

What the messages look like

Every message identifies Qintara Corp by name and relates only to an appointment the recipient booked.

Qintara Corp: your 15-minute intro call is confirmed for Tue Sep 8 at 2:00 PM CT. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg and data rates may apply.
Qintara Corp: reminder that your 15-minute intro call starts in one hour. Reply RESCHEDULE if you need a different time. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Frequency, opting out, and help

  • Message frequency varies and depends on the appointments you book. In practice this is a confirmation and a reminder per appointment.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP (or CANCEL, QUIT, END, UNSUBSCRIBE, OPTOUT, STOPALL, REVOKE) to any message. You will get one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and then nothing further.
  • To start again: reply START.
  • For help: reply HELP or INFO, or email hello@qintaracorp.com.

The exact automated replies are:

HELP → Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Msg&Data Rates May Apply.
STOP → You have successfully been unsubscribed. You will not receive any more messages from this number. Reply START to resubscribe.
START → Qintara Corp: you are subscribed to appointment confirmations and reminders. Msg frequency varies. Msg and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
